# Week 2: Foundations of data visualisation

This week introduces the foundations of data visualisation. We will work with behavioural data, create exploratory plots, and discuss why visualisation is useful for noticing patterns, variation, outliers, and potentially misleading summaries.

The session introduces core principles of effective visualisation and uses examples such as Anscombe's quartet and the Datasaurus Dozen to show why summary statistics alone are not enough. The practical work focuses on creating and interpreting basic plots in R.

By the end of Week 2, you should be able to:

- explain why visualisation is important for behavioural data,
- create basic exploratory plots in R,
- describe how different plots can reveal or hide patterns,
- identify examples of misleading or unclear visualisation,
- begin applying basic design principles to simple figures.
