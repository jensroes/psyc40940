# Week 3 Notes For Next Year

- Timing in 2026 was comfortable. There was a little extra room in the session, but this was not a problem.
- For next year, consider adding a small amount of extra discussion or one short concept-check activity, but there is no pressure to expand the session.
- The two-exercise structure worked well. Keep Exercise 1 focused on layers and Exercise 2 focused on aesthetics versus attributes.
- Avoid asking students to choose how to improve plot readability before they have learned enough options. Reflection questions should focus on what changed when layers were added.
- Avoid `geom_quantile()` in student-facing Week 3 materials because it can require installing `quantreg`. Use `stat_smooth(method = "lm", se = FALSE)` for the early trend-line example instead.
- Keep using `stat_smooth()` rather than `geom_smooth()` for consistency across slides and exercises.
