# Week 10: Reproduce and review your portfolio

This session is for consolidation and individual project support. We will check that the report and separate Shiny app run from the submitted files, test an extracted ZIP, and review the portfolio against the assessment requirements.

Download and unzip the whole Week 10 folder. Knit `01_reproduce_and_review_your_portfolio.Rmd` first and read the HTML guide. Bring your own project if ready; the supplied submission-example/ project is available for practising the checks.

There is no new compulsory Shiny feature. Most of the session is for building, debugging and improving your project, including questions, visualisations, app explanations and reflection. Bring specific questions or errors, or your candidate dataset if you still need help with suitability.

Aim to have a working report and app before the winter break. Use the current assessment brief for the deadline, word limit, anonymity, AI declaration and full submission instructions.
